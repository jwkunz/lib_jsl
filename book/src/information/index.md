# Information theory: source coding, LFSRs, checksums and hashes

Information theory asks how compactly data can be described and how reliably it can be sent. This chapter covers the first question and its neighbours: the measures of information, the lossless compressors that approach
those limits, the shift-register sequences that look random but are generated by a few gates, and the checksums and hashes that detect corruption. The [error-correcting codes](../coding/index.md) of the next chapter take up the second question.

## The central questions

- **How much information is there?** The entropy of a source bounds lossless compression; real data's structure between symbols makes its true entropy rate far lower than its symbol entropy.
- **Model or dictionary?** Entropy coders need the symbol probabilities; dictionary coders learn repeated strings without any model, and practical compressors combine the two.
- **Random or structured?** LFSR sequences are perfectly predictable yet have the balance and correlation of noise, which is what ranging and spread spectrum need.
- **Accident or adversary?** A CRC catches the errors a channel makes; only a cryptographic hash resists someone choosing the errors.

## Testing against Python and SciPy

The references come from Python 3.13's `zlib`, `hashlib`, `bz2` and `lzma` modules and from SciPy 1.17.1, by `book/generator/data/make_information_refs.py`. The entropy and divergences agree with `scipy.stats.entropy` to 12 digits, the CRC-32 and SHA-256 values are identical on every test input, and all six compressors
reproduce the 4.6 MB test text exactly. 

## In the library

All of these are in `lib_jsl_information` ([API reference](../../api/lib_jsl_information/index.html)).

| You need | Use | Entry |
|---|---|---|
| Entropy, cross entropy, divergence or mutual information; a Gray code | `entropy`, `entropy_from_probabilities`, `cross_entropy`, `kl_divergence`, `mutual_information`, `gray_code` | [Entropy and information](entropy.md) |
| Compression by symbol frequencies | `huffman`, `shannon_fano`, `arithmetic` (or the `SourceCoder` wrappers) | [Entropy coding](entropy-coding.md) |

| A checksum or a cryptographic digest | `crc32`, `crc32c`, `Crc32`, `sha256`, `Sha256` | [Checksums and hashes](checksums.md) |
